BIORESONANCE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bioresonance (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use the Bioresonance mobile application (the “App”). Our personalized rituals are designed to run on your device. Purchases, technical diagnostics and optional advertising measurement involve the providers described below.

Privacy at a glance
  • Your ritual descriptions, photos, AI personalization and saved ritual history are processed locally. We do not send this content to advertising services.
  • In version 1.1.0, the App requests on-device speech recognition on iOS and Android, and does not switch to cloud transcription when that recognition is unavailable. You can type your request instead and skip the photo.
  • Apple or Google and RevenueCat handle purchases, subscription entitlement and related technical and usage information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Google ML Kit process technical diagnostics. This is separate from the optional advertising choice.
  • Google and YouTube campaign measurement is off until you agree. It also requires Apple’s tracking permission on iOS. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy.

1. Your ritual and on-device processing

The App processes the text you enter about your needs, the context and your desired state; optional microphone input and transcription; an optional photo; voice waveform and harmonic measurements; local AI output; selected programs and sequences; and saved session history and preferences. These features do not require a Bioresonance account.

The AI model runs on your device. The App does not intentionally upload your ritual descriptions, transcription, photos, AI results, selected frequency sequences or saved ritual history to Bioresonance, Firebase or RevenueCat. These contents are not supplied as advertising event parameters. Operating-system services and your own device backup settings remain separate from the App’s processing.

If you add a photo, Google ML Kit performs face detection on the device to obtain limited visual measurements, such as capture quality, eye openness and alignment. This is not facial identification, a medical assessment or a remote analysis of the photo. Technical SDK information is described in section 3.

In version 1.1.0, microphone transcription uses the system’s on-device speech recognizer. If the device, language or permissions do not support it, voice input may be unavailable; the App does not silently fall back to a remote speech service. You can complete the personalized ritual using text. Earlier Android versions could use a default speech service with an offline preference that did not guarantee local processing; that service’s privacy practices applied to any remote transcription.

2. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We do not receive complete payment-card details.

RevenueCat presents subscription offers, validates and restores purchases, and determines which subscription features are available. It processes a generated pseudonymous App User ID, product and transaction identifiers, receipts or purchase tokens, purchase and subscription history, entitlement status, store and platform information, and technical request information. RevenueCat’s subscription interface can record offer views and related interactions for usage analysis.

RevenueCat also determines a country using transaction information or, when needed, an IP-derived country. RevenueCat states that the IP address is discarded after determining that country. The generated customer ID is not your name or email address, but it is an identifier and should not be understood as fully anonymous data. Your ritual content is not sent to RevenueCat.

3. Reliability and technical SDK information

Firebase Crashlytics is enabled in store releases to help identify and resolve failures. Google processes crash stack traces and relevant application state, app and SDK versions, device and operating-system information, timestamps, installation and session identifiers, and associated technical diagnostics. When optional Analytics is enabled, Crashlytics can also receive Analytics event breadcrumbs that help explain activity preceding a crash.

We do not deliberately attach your ritual descriptions, voice, photos, selected programs or AI output as crash-report logs, custom fields or user identifiers. Technical diagnostics are separate from advertising measurement and are not disabled by declining the advertising choice.

Google ML Kit on both iOS and Android may send technical information to Google, including device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, feature-use events, errors, and configuration information such as image format and dimensions. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics. The face-detection feature processes the image locally; these technical exchanges do not upload the photo.

The local ritual diagnostic journal used during development is unavailable in App Store and Google Play release builds. An earlier development preference cannot enable that journal in a store release.

4. Advertising attribution and your choice

Optional Google and YouTube campaign measurement

This release does not display advertisements inside the App. The optional measurement concerns advertisements promoting Bioresonance elsewhere, including on Google and YouTube.

Google Analytics for Firebase collection starts only after your affirmative choice. On iOS, the App additionally requires App Tracking Transparency authorization for this measurement. Declining does not restrict the App’s features or change your subscription. Advertising personalization is disabled in our measurement configuration.

When enabled, Google Analytics processes an app-instance identifier, available advertising or device identifiers, technical app and device information, general location derived from a masked IP address, app lifecycle and engagement events, and supported automatic in-app purchase and subscription events, which can include product identifiers, names and prices. The App also records an attempt to open a subscription offer. Automatic screen-name reporting is disabled; ritual text, symptoms, photos, voice, chosen programs and local diagnostic records are not sent as commercial event data.

Our Analytics property is linked to Google Ads for campaign measurement. Permitted measurement data can help connect app installations and commercial interactions to advertisements. With the same permission, RevenueCat receives the Firebase app-instance identifier and your measurement choice, version of that choice and measurement environment, linking these preferences to its subscription record. This does not make your ritual content available to Google Ads or RevenueCat.

You can refuse or withdraw this permission in Settings → Privacy. Withdrawal disables Analytics collection on the device, resets its Analytics identifier and queues the updated choice for RevenueCat. If the device is offline, the provider receives that update when synchronization is possible. Withdrawal does not automatically delete data already received, cancel a subscription or disable purchase validation and technical diagnostics. On iOS, you can also change tracking permission in the device’s Settings.

Apple Ads attribution on iOS

Since iOS version 1.0.2, a separate integration uses Apple’s AdServices framework. RevenueCat receives an attribution token and requests available attribution information from Apple, such as campaign, ad group, campaign keyword, ad, country or region, and installation or ad-interaction type. RevenueCat associates this information with its customer and subscription records to measure our Apple campaign performance. Campaign keywords describe our advertisements; they are not the words you enter for your ritual.

This Apple attribution integration does not itself request App Tracking Transparency authorization or access the IDFA. It is separate from the optional Google measurement choice above. Your ritual descriptions, voice, photos, AI output and saved session history are not sent for this attribution.

5. Permissions and support

The App requests microphone and speech-recognition permissions when you use voice input, and camera permission when you choose to take a photo. You can type instead of using the microphone and skip the photo. Permissions can be changed in your device settings.

If you contact support, we process the contact information and message you choose to send so that we can respond. Please do not send payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Purposes and legal bases

Where applicable, we rely on performance of a contract to provide requested features, purchases and restoration; legitimate interests to secure the service, prevent abuse, provide support and improve technical reliability; consent for optional advertising measurement and other processing requiring consent; and legal obligations concerning transactions and consumer protection. System permissions and the App’s advertising choice are distinct controls.

7. Retention and deletion

Local content remains on your device until you remove it in the App, clear app data or uninstall, subject to applicable device backup settings. Saved sessions can be deleted in the App. Android’s app backup is disabled in this release.

We retain provider-held information only as needed for the purposes described above and applicable legal obligations. Analytics event and user-data retention follows our property settings and Google’s service policies; aggregated reports can have different retention. Firebase states that Crashlytics crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins. Subscription and transaction information may be kept longer for accounting, fraud prevention, restoration and legal obligations. Support information is kept as necessary to handle the request and relevant follow-up.

Deleting local app data or withdrawing consent does not automatically erase provider-held records or cancel a subscription. Contact us to request deletion of information we can identify and control; we may need enough information to locate the relevant subscription or technical record, and some records must be retained where legally required. Manage subscription cancellation through the relevant app store.

8. Providers and international processing

We do not sell your personal information. Apple, Google, Firebase and RevenueCat process the limited information described in this Policy, including optional advertising measurement with Google. These providers may process information outside your country and use contractual and other legally required safeguards for international transfers. An optional measurement choice does not authorize us to send your ritual content to advertising services.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal. You may exercise the optional advertising choice in Settings → Privacy without losing access to the App.

Most ritual content is held only on your device and is not accessible to us. You can remove that content directly. For provider-held information or a privacy request, use the contact below. Apple and Google control parts of the store transaction record and may also need to handle requests relating to those records.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires a higher age for independent consent, a parent or legal guardian must authorize use. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

11. Security and changes

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. The providers described above use encrypted network connections for their app services. No device, software or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and updated.

We may update this Policy when the App, providers or relevant law changes. The date above identifies the latest revision; material changes may also be communicated in the App or store listing.

12. Contact

Bioresonance is responsible for this Privacy Policy. For privacy questions and deletion requests, contact bioresonance528hz (arobase) gmail.com. You can also use the App Support contact in the App Store or Google Play listing.
