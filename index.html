Questions about Bioresonance

What is the Bioresonance app?

Bioresonance: Frequency Sounds is an app by Boris Douarre for personal sound rituals. It contains more than 10,000 Rife frequency sequences and 1,500 ready-to-use programs. It is designed for relaxation and everyday wellbeing.

How does a personalized ritual work?

Describe your current situation and intention by voice or text. The app processes this input on your device and selects 1 to 5 existing frequency sequences. You can also choose a program or create your own selection.

Can I use Bioresonance offline?

Ritual personalization and audio playback work on your device after installation. Subscription purchases and some technical services require a connection. Android speech transcription depends on the device’s speech service and settings.

Do I need headphones?

Headphones are not required for the core frequency tones: you can use your device speaker or an external speaker. Optional binaural sound layers need headphones to be heard as intended.

Where can I download it and which languages are supported?

Download Bioresonance from the App Store for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 16.6 or later. The app supports English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. The Android version is coming soon.

Is Bioresonance a medical treatment?

No. Bioresonance is designed for relaxation and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease and does not replace medical advice or treatment.
