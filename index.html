Common questions

What will I hear?

Pure, steady tones. You can add background sounds if you prefer.

Can I choose without AI?

Yes. Browse the programs or build your own selection from the library.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for personalization and playback after installation. Purchases and some technical services need a connection.

Do I need headphones?

Only for binaural backgrounds. Listen at a low volume and stop if you feel uncomfortable.

What about Android?

The Android version is coming soon. Voice input may rely on the device’s speech service, which can process speech remotely.

Is it a medical treatment?

No. Bioresonance is designed for relaxation and wellbeing. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease and does not replace medical advice or treatment.
